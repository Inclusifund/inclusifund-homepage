03 · The sort

Four kinds of people are in your comments.

Each one needs something different. Treat them all the same and you either undersell the experienced ones or overwhelm the beginners.

A

Contract-ready

Registered with CQC or on a framework, delivering already. Wants the next contract.

→ Bid support on live tenders
B

Operator

Has managed teams or services. Knows the work and wants to own the business.

→ Set-up, registration, first contracts
C

Builder

Frontline care experience. Ready to start, doesn't know the route yet.

→ Guided path + group mentoring
D

Explorer

Never worked in care. Curious, inspired, early.

→ Community, guides, learn the industry

05 · Where the system lives

Stan Store stays. It just can't be the whole house.

Stan is a good till. It isn't built to hold a database, an intake form or a sorting engine, and it can't run on a domain you own.

Stan Store, as it is today

  • ✓ Checkout, digital products, mailing list sign-up
  • ✓ Sends new customers out through Zapier
  • ✗ No custom domain. It stays at stan.store/… (Stan's own help page)
  • ✗ No database you control, no custom forms, no sorting

What we'd add

  • ✓ Your own domain, with intake, sorting and the member area living there
  • ✓ A database you own: every person, their tier and their history
  • ✓ Stan keeps selling, and every new customer flows straight into the system
Your posts→Your domain→Sort→Stan checkout

For today: do you already own a domain? If not, it's around £10 a year and stays in your name.
