Frequently Asked Questions

Who is InclusiFund for?

UK grassroots community organisations — CICs (Community Interest Companies), small charities, and community-run businesses, especially those below the £1M income threshold where most institutional funding stops looking.

What does InclusiFund actually do?

We pair grant-matching and application-writing with building the tools those operators need — websites, director dashboards, and content systems. AntiGravity Projects is our build arm that delivers the technology side.

Who runs InclusiFund?

InclusiGroup Ltd is the registered company; InclusiFund is the brand. Founder: Reece Dunn. Contact: reece.dunn@inclusifund.co.uk.

What are the related InclusiFund surfaces?

studio.inclusifund.co.uk (portfolio + studio landing pages), aiq.inclusifund.co.uk (AI fluency assessment), community.inclusifund.co.uk (member community), and tools.inclusifund.co.uk (the CIC toolkit).
